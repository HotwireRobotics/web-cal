Outlook, Android, and other calendar apps
  • Outlook: Add calendar → Subscribe from web (or From internet), then paste the link above.
  • Android: subscribe with Google Calendar on a computer at calendar.google.com (Other calendars → + → From URL). It then syncs to your phone.
  • Anything else: look for “subscribe”, “add by URL”, or “internet calendar” and paste the link.
  • Changes show up on the calendar app's own schedule: Apple and Outlook usually within an hour or two, Google Calendar can take up to a day.
